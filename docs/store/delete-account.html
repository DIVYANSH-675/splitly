On-device ledgers for younger users

On-device mode has no cloud account. Its groups, names, bills and parent-control settings are stored only on the device; we cannot retrieve or delete them remotely. A parent or guardian can remove local groups in the app, or clear SplitBill app data in Android settings to remove the local ledger and parent controls. Uninstalling also removes local app data. This is permanent and there is no cloud backup for this mode. The temporary adult demo clears when you exit it.

What happens next for cloud accounts

  1. We verify that you own the account. If you cannot access the original Google email, contact the same support address for help verifying ownership. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, bank details or identity documents in your initial request.
  2. After verification, we aim to complete deletion within 30 days and confirm it to you by email.
  3. The request covers your account, profile, linked sign-in identities and sessions, uploaded attachments, and your identifying information and personal records in shared groups and history. Shared records and balances may change. Reports already downloaded or copied by other people cannot be recalled. We will explain any specific legal or security retention that applies.

You can also request removal of particular personal data without closing your entire account by describing it in an email to the same address.

After your request is completed, clear local app data on your devices. This process deletes your SplitBill account, not your Google account. Simply uninstalling the app does not delete cloud data.
