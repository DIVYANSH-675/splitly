Privacy policy

SplitBill is provided by Divyansh007. For privacy questions, contact developerg675@gmail.com.

Information we receive

When you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, verified email address, name and profile photo. You can edit your SplitBill name, username and optional photo.

We store the groups, member names, bills, dates, amounts, categories, shares, transfers, optional receipts and files, and activity records you provide. Group members can see their group's shared records. Username search lets people find accounts to add to a group.

How information is used

We use this information to sign you in, restore your synced groups, calculate balances, share group records, sync offline changes and create requested exports. SplitBill records transfers; it does not transfer money or connect to your bank.

Google provides sign-in. Supabase hosts authentication, the API and database. These providers may process operational data, such as IP addresses and request logs, to operate and secure their services. Information may be processed outside your country. See Google's privacy policy and Supabase's privacy policy.

The QR scanner uses Google ML Kit. It processes camera frames on your device and may send device and app information, installation identifiers, scanner events, configuration and performance/error metrics to Google for diagnostics and usage analytics. See Google ML Kit data disclosures.

SplitBill has no advertising SDK and does not sell your data. Shared group information is visible to people in that group. When you export or share a report, the recipients and saved copies are under your control.

Device storage and permissions

SplitBill caches account and group data and pending changes on your device for offline use. It uses the camera only when you choose to scan a group QR code, and the system photo or file picker for optional profile images and receipts. It does not request contacts, precise location, microphone access or bank access.

Cloud requests use HTTPS. Access to group records and attachments requires group membership. Protect your device and Google account; no service can promise absolute security.

Retention and deletion

Account and group records remain while you use SplitBill, until you remove eligible records or request account deletion. You can request account and associated data deletion without reinstalling the app. We verify ownership and aim to complete requests within 30 days after verification.

A verified deletion request covers your SplitBill account, profile, sign-in identities and sessions, uploaded attachments, and your identifying information and personal records held in shared groups and activity history. Deletion can change shared records and balances. We cannot recall files already exported or copied by other people. Provider-managed backups or security logs may remain for their normal retention period; we will explain any specific legal or security retention that applies to your request.

Deleting the cloud account does not remotely erase another device's cache. After pending changes are handled, sign out and clear the app's local data or uninstall it. Deleting SplitBill does not delete your Google account.

Your choices and policy changes

You can edit your profile, choose whether to attach files, and choose whom to share groups and exports with. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your account data. Material policy changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
